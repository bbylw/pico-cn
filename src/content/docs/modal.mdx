模态框以 `<dialog>` 作为包裹容器，`<article>` 承载模态框内容。

`<header>` 内的 `<button rel="prev">` 被定义为 `float: right;`，让关闭图标能够与标题顶部对齐。

<Ex
  html={`<dialog class="example" open=""><article><header><button aria-label="Close" rel="prev"></button><p><strong>🗓️ Thank You for Registering!</strong></p></header><p>We’re excited to have you join us for our upcoming event. Please arrive at the museum on time to check in and get started.</p><ul><li>Date: Saturday, April 15</li><li>Time: 10:00am - 12:00pm</li></ul></article></dialog>`}
  code={`<dialog open>
  <article>
    <header>
      <button aria-label="Close" rel="prev"></button>
      <p>
        <strong>🗓️ Thank You for Registering!</strong>
      </p>
    </header>
    <p>
      We're excited to have you join us for our
      upcoming event. Please arrive at the museum 
      on time to check in and get started.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Date: Saturday, April 15</li>
      <li>Time: 10:00am - 12:00pm</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
</dialog>`}
  lang="html"

/>

`<footer>` 内的内容默认右对齐。

Please review the membership details below:</p><ul><li>Membership: Individual</li><li>Price: $10</li></ul><footer><button class="secondary">Cancel</button><button>Confirm</button></footer></article></dialog>`}

<h2 id="demo">演示</h2>

点击下方按钮切换模态框。

<Raw html={`<article aria-label="Modal demo" id="modal-demo"><button class="contrast">Open modal</button></article>`} />

Pico 不包含 JavaScript 代码。你需要自行编写 JS 来与模态框交互。

作为一个起点，你可以参考 [HTMLDialogElement](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLDialogElement)，或查看下面的进阶示例：

- [原生 JavaScript](https://codesandbox.io/embed/4mrnhq?view=Editor+%2B+Preview&module=%2Fjs%2Fmodal.js)
- [React](https://codesandbox.io/p/devbox/github/picocss/examples/tree/master/v2-react-color-schemes-and-modal?embed=1&file=%2Fsrc%2Fcomponents%2FModal.js)

要打开模态框，在 `<dialog>` 容器上添加 `open` 属性。

<h2 id="utilities">工具类</h2>

模态框附带 3 个工具类。

这些工具类在[无类版本](/docs/classless/)中不可用。

`.modal-is-open` 可阻止模态框下方的任何滚动和交互。

```html
<!doctype html>
<html class="modal-is-open">
  ...
</html>
```

`.modal-is-opening` 提供打开动画。

```html
<!doctype html>
<html class="modal-is-open modal-is-opening">
  ...
</html>
```

`.modal-is-closing` 提供关闭动画。

```html
<!doctype html>
<html class="modal-is-open modal-is-closing">
  ...
</html>
```
